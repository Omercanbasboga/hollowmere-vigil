One honest note on quality: the Shadow Warden portrait is the weakest match of the three classes,
no CC0 pack scanned had a clean hooded-rogue sprite as good as the knight or the mage, so it's
using the closest thing available (reads more like an elder or druid than a rogue). The Grave
Moth sprite is a single moth, not a swarm, no CC0 "swarm of small moths" sprite exists as far as
this search turned up, recolored from its original teal toward purple with a CSS filter since
the source color didn't match. The Fen Serpent sprite is a simple Snake-game-style green snake,
not a naturalistic illustration, it's the plainest of the five creature picks but it's what's out
there under a usable license.
